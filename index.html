Chenjia (Chen) Lin

Welcome to my webpage! I am a third-year statistics PhD student advised by Prof. Li Ma at the University of Chicago. I am broadly interested in density ratio estimation and scientific applications of generative models.

I earned a B.S. in Mathematics and B.A. in Statistics from the University of Chicago in June 2023. Before starting graduate school, I was a research assistant at the Jerison Lab in the Department of Physics.
